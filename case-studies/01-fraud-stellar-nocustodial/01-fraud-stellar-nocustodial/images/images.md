## On-Chain Account View (Stellar Explorer)
Screenshot of a public Stellar block explorer showing the on-chain footprint of an account involved in a fraud investigation for a non-custodial Fintech application.
- Used to verify account activity, signer configuration, and the account's creation and funding structure.
- Shows a multi-signature setup (2/2/2 thresholds) and sponsored reserves, which help trace how an account was created and operated.
- Account address, signer keys, creator and sponsor accounts, and creation date are redacted for privacy.
