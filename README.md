## What This Portfolio Demonstrates

This portfolio covers the critical GRC and operational risk areas of a non-custodial fintech environment through six distinct case studies:

* **01 | KYB & Customer Due Diligence:** Payer–account holder mismatch and source-of-funds validation.
* **02 | Third-Party & Source of Funds:** Managing deposits originating from accounts that do not match the registered user.
* **03 | Application Security (IAM):** Account Takeover (ATO) prevention and access control via recovery email validation.
* **04 | Fraud & Incident Response:** Inter-organizational fraud coordination and escalation with independent anchors.
* **05 | Data Privacy & Protection:** Handling requests for recipient data and enforcing confidentiality under pressure.
* **06 | KYC Operations:** Managing third-party dependencies and process reliability in stuck KYC verifications.
* **07 | Incident Investigation:** Root cause analysis of a misdirected transfer, testing control effectiveness, and incident triage.
* **08 | Sanctions & Geoblocking:** Handling international sanctions enforcement by third-party providers, error triage, and UX compliance messaging.
* **09 | Cross-Chain Operations:** Managing unsupported network routing and preventing user error in cross-chain transfers.
